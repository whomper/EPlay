EPlay includes or uses, under their own licences (compatible with the GPL):

- data/mp3huff.txt and data/mp3window.txt: the Huffman codes and the
  synthesis window of MPEG audio layer III (ISO/IEC 11172-3, Annex B), as
  FFmpeg transcribes them (libavcodec/mpegaudiodec_common.c and
  mpegaudiodsp_data.c, GNU LGPL 2.1 or later), extracted by tools/mkhuff.py
  and tools/mkwin.py.

- third_party/minimp3/minimp3.h: minimp3 by Lion (lieff), CC0 1.0
  (third_party/minimp3/LICENSE). The tests' reference decoder, and the
  MP3 decoder of MP3FPU.OVL (a 68060 with its FPU and no Falcon DSP),
  unchanged; it is not part of EPLAY.PRG.

- common/third_party/tjpgd: TJpgDec R0.03, the Tiny JPEG Decompressor,
  Copyright (C) 2021, ChaN, all right reserved (elm-chan.org/fsw/tjpgd/),
  as the AERA family changed it (its README.txt). Its licence: "The TJpgDec
  module is a free software and there is NO WARRANTY. No restriction on use.
  You can use, modify and redistribute it for personal, non-profit or
  commercial products UNDER YOUR RESPONSIBILITY. Redistributions of source
  code must retain the above copyright notice." It decodes the album art's
  JPEGs (common/src/pic.c), in EPLAY.PRG.

- tls.ovl (common/tlsovl/): the AERA family's secure connections, for web
  radio stations at https addresses on a Direct connection. It is made with
  BearSSL 0.6 by Thomas Pornin (common/third_party/bearssl/, MIT licence:
  "Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person obtaining a
  copy of this software ... THE SOFTWARE IS PROVIDED "AS IS", WITHOUT
  WARRANTY OF ANY KIND"; the whole text in its LICENSE.txt).

- CACERT.PEM (common/certs/): Mozilla's root certificates, as curl's
  project publishes them, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
